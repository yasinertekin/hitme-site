Support

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Write to yasinertekinn@outlook.com. Please include your device model and, for a bug, what you were doing when it happened.

My run is gone

The run in progress is saved on your device after every move; CONTINUE on the title screen brings it back. Your progress (unlocks, achievements, records) is also backed up to your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google backup (Android).

Start over

Settings > RESET DATA clears your progress and saved run on this device.

Purchases and ads

Hit Me has no ads and no in-app purchases. It plays fully offline.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Türkçe

Hit Me is a single-player game. It does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. The game plays fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your saved run, your progress (unlocks, achievements, records, the Collection, your Daily Run history) and your settings are stored on this device only.

Your own cloud backup

On iPhone, your progress (not the run in progress) is also kept in your own iCloud account so it follows you to your other devices. On Android, your progress is included in your device's own Google backup if backup is turned on. These copies live in your Apple or Google account; the developer cannot see or access them.

On iPhone, achievements you earn are shown in Game Center under your Game Center account.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, an anonymous crash report may be sent (Unity Cloud Diagnostics) so the bug can be fixed: the error and where it happened in the game's code, the device model, the operating system version, the game version and a random install number. It contains no name, no contact details, no location and nothing you typed, it is not linked to you and it is never used for advertising or tracking.

Deleting your data

Settings > RESET DATA deletes your progress and saved run on this device (and, on iPhone, the iCloud copy is replaced by the reset profile). Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on the device.

Children

Hit Me is made for adults (it contains simulated gambling for points, with no real money) and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the game and on this page. Questions about privacy: yasinertekinn@outlook.com.
